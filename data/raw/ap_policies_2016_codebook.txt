AP Policies 2016 — Coding Key
Source: Education Commission of the States, Advanced Placement 2016 All Data Points (May 2016).

Binary coding:
1 = ECS explicitly documented a qualifying state policy/program in 2016.
0 = ECS did not document a qualifying state policy/program in 2016.

performance_incentive:
Explicit state money, funding, or award tied to successful AP exam performance (such as a score of 3+).
General AP funding or programs merely intended to improve performance are not coded 1.

participation_incentive:
Explicit state money or funding tied to AP enrollment, course completion, or exam-taking regardless of exam score.
General AP access/startup grants are not coded 1.

teacher_training:
ECS documents a state program, requirement, or funding for AP teacher training/professional development.

exam_fee_support:
ECS documents state subsidies/support for AP testing fees. Federal-only or local-only support is not sufficient.

access_support:
ECS documents state support intended to encourage or expand access to AP.

accountability:
ECS documents AP participation and/or success in state high-school accountability metrics or reporting.

policy_notes:
Short source-derived explanation for selected classifications. Blank does not mean that ECS reported no AP policy of any kind; it only means no additional note was needed for this six-variable analysis.

Important limitation:
These variables describe the ECS May 2016 policy snapshot. They should be treated as historical policy context when compared with 2025 College Board outcomes. The visualization can show associations/patterns, not causal effects.
