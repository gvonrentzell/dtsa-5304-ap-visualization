Understanding the measures

Participation: Share of the graduating class taking AP exams. Exam pass rate: Share of AP exams scoring 3 or higher.

High-score rate: Share of exams scoring 4 or 5. Exams per examinee: Average number of AP exams taken per AP examinee.

AP exams per projected graduate: Total AP exams administered in 2025 (across grade levels), divided by WICHE’s projected 2025 public-plus-private high school graduates. This is an annual exam-volume ratio, not the average exams taken by graduating seniors.

Sources: College Board (2025 AP outcomes); Education Commission of the States (2016 AP policy classifications); WICHE (2025 graduating-class projections, 11th edition). Policy presence does not establish that a policy caused an outcome. Map boundaries require internet access.
